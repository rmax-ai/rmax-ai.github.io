# Year One visual edition

Delegation Queue issue #345 grants an operator exception to the standard technical-note template for this retrospective. The existing article and its evidence appendix supply the text and evidence boundaries. The exception covers the article shell, local typography, components, figures and preview. Review, rasterization and the merge decision belong to the host and operator.

The masthead sets the full title in two levels of Inter with a system fallback. The edition opens with the reporting window and two counts on a pale violet strip. Both counts carry their units. The cutoff and the limit on verified delivery sit beside them. Readers reach the chapter links before the methodology. On the warm reading surface, prose fits a column of about 65 characters. The diagrams, project panels and priorities can use the width of the report.

The date rail is an ordered list in document order. Evidence labels travel with each milestone as the rail collapses on narrow screens. The appendix retains the eleven monthly entries. The architecture graphic follows a request across three responsibility boundaries and brings its reviewed revision back for a decision. Desktop uses columns and mobile uses bands. A solid local retry stays inside execution. The dashed proposed follow-up leads to a new issue. The numbered key explains who acts and what gets recorded.

The task chart retains its audited denominator and guard. Each proposed 2027 priority has its own heading and definition list with the owner, baseline, first artifact, measure and safeguard. On a narrow screen those fields read as ordinary text in one column.

The editable preview source is `figures/social-preview.svg` under the public note. It uses the same masthead, coverage line and census strip at 1200 × 630. The host-generated sibling `social-preview.png` is the Open Graph image for the large-image social card. The preserved render evidence predates this edition.
